💡 How to Prevent & Reduce Air Pollution?

  • Use public transportation, carpool, or ride a bicycle instead of driving solo.
  • Avoid burning garbage, leaves, or plastic in open spaces.
  • Plant more trees in your neighborhood and support local green initiatives.
  • Switch to energy-efficient appliances and reduce energy consumption.

🏥 Possible Health Risks

Moderate air quality may cause respiratory discomfort for sensitive groups, including children and asthma patients. Consider wearing a mask outdoors.
